HYPOTHESIS
----------
Scripts detect the LADB serial dynamically (priority: *adb-tls-connect* ->
host:port -> any "device"), so they keep working unchanged when LADB
restarts and its serial changes — even across format change
(mDNS/TLS instead of host:port).

ACTION
------
1. Captured SERIAL_OLD via adb devices (nothing hardcoded).
2. Restarted the LADB app normally (no re-pairing, no settings change).
3. Polled adb devices until transport returned; captured SERIAL_NEW.
4. Compared serials mechanically.
5. Ran unmodified scripts: doctor.sh -> recover.sh -> verify.sh.
6. Re-verified script hashes after the run.
7. No watchdog, no reboot, no re-pairing, no forced kill, no extra experiments.
